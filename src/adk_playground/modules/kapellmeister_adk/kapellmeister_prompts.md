# Kapellmeister — Prompt Guide

This document collects **recommended prompts** for testing and evaluating the Kapellmeister agents.  
Prompts are grouped by capability and agent type.

## 🎻 1. Composer Explainer (Kapellmeister Explainer Agent)

### Basic composer queries
Who was Beethoven?

Tell me about Ludwig van Beethoven in simple terms.

Give me a short introduction to Dvořák.

What kind of music did Debussy write?

### Style, era & comparisons
Was Brahms classical or romantic?

Explain the difference between Mozart and Beethoven in terms of musical style.

Compare Ravel and Debussy for a beginner audience.

### Mixed / chained queries
Tell me who Beethoven was and then explain his Symphony No. 5.

Give me a quick overview of Dvořák and list 2 of his most important works.

## 🎼 2. Work Explainer (Kapellmeister Explainer Agent)

### Basic work queries
What is Beethoven’s Symphony No. 5?

Explain Symphony No. 9 by Dvořák.

Describe Debussy’s Clair de lune.

### Context-focused prompts
Why is Beethoven’s Symphony No. 5 considered important?

Explain Mozart’s Requiem to someone new to classical music.

Give me interesting facts about Beethoven's Fifth Symphony.

### Ambiguous requests
Tell me about the piece called "Nocturne in Blue".

Explain Piano Quartet No. 2.

## 🧪 3. Tool Selection Stress Tests

### Prefer composer tool
Give me a short biography of Brahms.

What was Schubert known for?

Who was Sibelius?

### Prefer work tool
What is Dvořák’s New World Symphony?

Explain Beethoven’s Symphony No. 5 in simple terms.

Describe Ravel’s Boléro.

### Ambiguous prompts
Tell me about Beethoven and his Fifth Symphony.

Give me an overview of Debussy and Clair de lune.

## 🎬 4. Multi-turn interaction prompts

Earlier you explained Beethoven. Now tell me about one of his famous works.

We talked about Beethoven's symphonies. Recommend another composer I might enjoy.

Continue our discussion about Romantic era music.

## 🚧 5. Future: Program Planning (placeholder)

Create a 60–70 minute concert program for beginners featuring Beethoven.

Plan a calm, introspective program for a chamber ensemble.

Give me a short concert program with one overture, one concerto, and one symphony.

## 📝 6. Debug Prompts

Show me which tool you decided to use and why.

Explain step-by-step how you picked the composer tool.

List the arguments you are passing to the tool call.

Don't answer directly — just tell me which tool you would use.
