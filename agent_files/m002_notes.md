# Beethoven – Symphony No. 5 (C minor), Op. 67

Fate motif: short-short-short-long.
Premiered: 1808, Theater an der Wien, Vienna.
Structure: 4 movements; attacca into finale.
Orchestration: pairs of winds, trumpets, timpani, strings.
Motivic development dominates the first movement.
C minor to triumphant C major narrative arc.
Historical impact: emblem of symphonic drama.
Recommended recording: Kleiber (DG), Gardiner (Archiv).
Listening tip: follow the rhythmic cell across sections.
Duration: ~32 minutes.
